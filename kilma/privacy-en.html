Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy explains how the game “Kilma w Ghataha” (“the app”) handles your data. The app needs no account, and we run no servers that collect your data.

1. Data that stays on your device

Level progress, stars, statistics, streak and settings are stored on your device and, if iCloud is enabled, synced between your devices through Apple’s iCloud service. We cannot access them.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier IDFA if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from it, ad interaction data and diagnostics in order to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. We first ask for your consent with Google’s consent form, then show Apple’s tracking permission prompt. If you decline, you receive non-personalized ads.

More: How Google uses data. You can change your choice at any time in the app’s Settings or in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

3. Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details; the app only verifies your entitlements on-device with StoreKit.

4. Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, leaderboard scores and achievements are sent to Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

5. Notifications

The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. No external push service is used.

6. Analytics

The app does not send usage data to any analytics service.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

8. Your choices

You can remove game data by deleting the app and turning off its iCloud sync. For advertising data requests, contact Google. To reach us, see the support page.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will show the new date at the top of this page.
